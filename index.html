A few little questions.

Where is Cozy Nail & Head Spa located?

We're at 14030 Telge Rd, Suite C, Cypress, TX 77429. Tap Directions above for a map.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use any Book Appointment button to see the online booking page, or call (281) 758-2111 for help.

Do you offer more than nails?

Yes. In addition to manicures, nail art and pedicures, we offer lash extensions, Head Spa, massage, facials, brows and waxing services.

Do you have Head Spa treatments for first-time guests?

Our displayed first-visit Head Spa special offers 30% off selected 60-, 90- and 120-minute sessions. Please confirm current eligibility when booking.
